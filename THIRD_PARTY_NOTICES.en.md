The **full licence text** of every component is in [THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt](THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt) (generated by `packaging/licenses.py`; the build checks that what goes into the exe matches that list). The settings page of the exe opens these files as well.

## Art and assets of this repository

- **Pixel cats, the office, the props, the onboarding animations** (`office/`, `assets/chibicat/`, `assets/design/`): original to this repository and drawn by the code in it (`office/build/cats.js`, `office/office.html` and the generator scripts in those folders), distributed under the MIT licence together with the code. The morning, day and evening backgrounds of the office are drawn by the canvas code, not external images.
- **Application icon** (`assets/icon/`): generated with Grok (xAI), picked by the maintainer and cut with `make_icons.py`; used under the xAI terms of service. We offer it under the MIT licence to the extent we hold rights in it.
- **Promo clips** (`assets/promo/`): the frames are drawn by a script, the music is synthesised with numpy by `make_music.py`. The subtitles use Noto Sans CJK SC (SIL Open Font License 1.1); no font file is kept in the repository.
- **Screenshots** (`office/screenshots/`, `office/mockups/`): all of them are this application's own interface.

### pystray and LGPL-3.0

pystray is the only LGPL component. This is how we comply:

- The exe carries the full text of LGPL-3.0 and GPL-3.0 (the pystray section of THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt).
- We did not modify pystray: we use the original from PyPI (the version is listed in THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt), and its source is available both on <https://github.com/moses-palmer/pystray> and on PyPI.
- The full source of agentboxbox and the build script (`packaging/build_exe.ps1`) are public. You can swap in a modified pystray and build the exe again, following the “Development” section of the README; that is the “recombine with a modified library” the LGPL asks for.

agentboxbox calls or reads the following, but does not carry them: AI clients (Cursor, Claude Code, Codex, Kimi Code, MiniMax Code, Grok Bot, WorkBuddy, ZCode), Git (it only runs `git` commands to read commit records) and the API of the model provider you picked. Their licences and terms are between you and them.
